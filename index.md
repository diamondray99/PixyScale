## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### How is PixyScale different from online rulers?
Online rulers use generic DPI values and don't account for your specific screen. PixyScale calibrates to each monitor's actual DPI, ensuring 1cm on screen = 1cm measured with a physical ruler.

### Does it work on multiple monitors with different DPIs?
Yes! PixyScale detects each monitor's DPI separately. When you drag the ruler to another screen, it automatically recalibrates for accurate measurements.

### What software does PixyScale work with?
PixyScale works as an overlay on any Windows application: Blender, ZBrush, AutoCAD, Revit, Figma, Adobe Creative Cloud, and more. It's not tied to any specific software.

### How accurate are the measurements?
When properly calibrated, PixyScale provides pixel-perfect accuracy matching physical rulers. DPI detection depends on Windows reporting correct monitor specifications.

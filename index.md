# PixyScale 📏  
### DPI-Accurate On-Screen Ruler for Professional Workflows

PixyScale is a **DPI-aware, multi-monitor on-screen ruler for Windows** that matches **real-world physical measurements exactly**.

Unlike online rulers or screenshot-based tools, PixyScale is built for **accuracy, consistency, and repeated professional use** — especially on high-DPI and multi-monitor setups.

---

## 🚩 The Problem

Most screen measurement tools fail in real workflows:

- ❌ Online rulers ignore screen DPI  
- ❌ Screenshot tools break on multi-monitor setups  
- ❌ Physical rulers on screens are inaccurate and impractical  

For professionals who need **real-world scale on digital screens**, these tools are unreliable.

---

## ✅ The Solution: PixyScale

PixyScale uses **per-screen DPI calibration** to ensure:

- **1 cm on screen = 1 cm in real life**
- Accurate measurements across monitors with different DPI values
- A lightweight, always-available on-screen workflow tool

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📐 DPI-accurate calibration (matches physical ruler measurements)
- 🖥 Multi-monitor support with per-screen recalibration
- 📏 Units: centimeters, millimeters, inches
- 🔄 Horizontal & vertical orientations
- 🔃 Reverse measurement direction
- 🎚 Adjustable opacity (20%–100%)
- 📊 Multiple rulers simultaneously
- 🎨 Themes: Light, Dark, Blueprint
- 🧭 System tray control for easy management

---
